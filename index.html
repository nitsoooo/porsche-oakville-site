The connection

It started with a 944 Turbo back in ’86Black on black.

I was six.
My father brought
home a Porsche.

That black-on-black 944 Turbo was the beginning. Attending Porsche Cup races in the late 1990s deepened the connection. In my memories of those circuits, the 951 looms largest.

Cars and motorsport became part of the bond I shared with my father. Porsche wasn’t simply a brand I admired. It was part of our story.

The 951

The car at the heart of the story.

944 Turbo / Personal archive

A project on pause.
A connection that isn’t.

My father parked the 951 in 1994. It was never driven again. Later, I bought it from him with the intention of restoring it—so I could share those same driving experiences with him and my two sons.

He passed away six years ago after a long battle with cancer. Life intervened, and the restoration had to be put on hold.

The cars were never just cars. They were a connection to my father, and something I wanted to pass on.

Making a premium idea tangible.
